COMPANION PAGE: AVIDAA GLOBAL maps who holds power; this page documents what the record says they did. Same people appear on both; the pages are deliberately separate.

METHODOLOGY: Every case here is sourced to a court conviction, government-commissioned audit/inquiry (e.g. Zondo Commission, SIGAR/SIGIR), regulatory action (DOJ, SEC, FCA), ICIJ-verified document leak, or -- for state violence -- an international court or treaty body (ICC warrant/custody, ICJ order, OPCW attribution, UN OHCHR or UN commission finding). This is a curated selection of the most significant, best-documented cases of the last decade -- not an exhaustive global registry, which does not exist and could not be verified to AVIDAA's standard if attempted. Status badges: DOCUMENTED = court conviction, government audit, or verified leak. REPORTED = credible ongoing investigation/indictment, conviction not yet final. No case is included on allegation alone. STATE VIOLENCE entries say plainly where accountability stops: a warrant that cannot be served, a finding with no tribunal, a conviction of the hands but not the head. WHISTLEBLOWERS are included as their own category: the entry documents what they exposed, what a court later ruled about it, and what happened to them -- including where courts vindicated the leak and punished the leaker anyway. A few entries (Snowden, Manning, Assange) originate before 2016 but are included because their decisive legal outcomes -- convictions, commutations, citizenship grants, plea deals, appellate rulings -- fall inside the 2016-2026 window.
